Global Network

Academic Collaborators & Partner Institutions

Solving grand challenges in cold molecular ion spectroscopy and quantum reaction dynamics requires close synergy between experimental AMO groups and theoretical quantum chemistry teams worldwide.

United Kingdom

University of Liverpool

Department of Physics — Collaborative research on linear Paul ion trapping, Velocity Map Imaging (VMI) detector optics, and cold ion–molecule reaction dynamics with Prof. Brianna Heazlewood's group.

Cold Ion Chemistry Coulomb Crystals VMI Instrumentation
United Kingdom

University of Oxford

Department of Chemistry / Physical & Theoretical Chemistry Laboratory — Scientific ties and foundational work on sympathetic cooling, resonance-enhanced photodissociation, and quantum capture dynamics.

Reaction Dynamics Photodissociation Quantum Capture
India · Host Institution

School of Physical & Chemical Sciences, IACS Kolkata

In-house collaborations with ab initio quantum chemistry and ultrafast spectroscopy groups at IACS for computing potential energy surfaces (PES), vibrational anharmonicities, and non-adiabatic couplings of molecular ions.

Ab Initio PES Quantum Scattering Laser Spectroscopy
India · National Network

Indian AMO & Quantum Technology Consortium

Active academic exchange across premier Indian research institutes (TIFR, IISER Kolkata, RRI, PRL, and IITs) in trapped-ion quantum control, precision spectroscopy, and cold atom/ion physics.

Trapped Ions Precision Metrology Student Exchange
