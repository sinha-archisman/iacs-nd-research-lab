Principal Investigator

Dr. Nabanita Deb

Faculty, School of Physical Sciences · Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science (IACS), Kolkata

Dr. Nabanita Deb leads the Cold Ion & Molecular Spectroscopy Group at IACS Kolkata. Prior to joining the faculty at IACS, she conducted pioneering research in laser-cooled ion trapping, Coulomb crystals, and cold ion-molecule reaction dynamics in leading international laboratories including the University of Oxford and the University of Liverpool (UK).

Her research program focuses on combining radiofrequency linear Paul traps with high-resolution Velocity Map Imaging (VMI) and laser spectroscopy to study quantum-state-selected molecular ions and astrochemically important reactions at millikelvin temperatures.
