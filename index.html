Core Capabilities

Molecular Physics & Chemical Dynamics

We integrate ultra-high-vacuum molecular beams, quadrupole and multipole ion optics, laser-induced rovibrational cooling, and coincidence Velocity Map Imaging to probe quantum-state-resolved molecular ions.

BBR-Assisted Rovibrational Laser Cooling

We develop optical pumping and blackbody-radiation (BBR)-assisted laser cooling schemes for non-linear symmetric-top cations (NH₃⁺, ND₃⁺) and linear triatomic cations (CO₂⁺, OCS⁺) exhibiting Renner-Teller vibronic coupling, compressing internal population into single rovibrational ground states.

NH₃⁺ & ND₃⁺ Renner-Teller Coupling Rovibrational Cooling

Dual-Sided Velocity Map Imaging (VMI)

Our custom dual-sided VMI spectrometer enables simultaneous positive ion and electron/negative ion coincidence detection using synchronized high-speed cameras and real-time sub-pixel centroiding to reconstruct complete 3D momentum vectors.

Coincidence Detection Sub-Pixel Centroiding 3D Momentum Mapping

Quadrupole Mass Filters & Multipole Ion Optics

We model and experimentally characterize non-linear resonances, Mathieu stability diagrams, and higher-order hexapole/octupole potential expansions in symmetric and asymmetric Quadrupole Mass Filters (QMF) for high-transmission mass selection.

Mathieu Stability Non-Linear Resonances SIMION Ion Optics

Cold Collisions & Penning Ionization Dynamics

Using supersonic molecular beams equipped with Even-Lavie pulsed valves and Dielectric Barrier Discharge (DBD) plasma sources, we generate metastable rare-gas beams (He*) to study Penning ionization and chemi-ionization at low collision energies.

Metastable He* Beams Even-Lavie + DBD Penning Ionization

Scientific Themes

Cold Molecular Ion Spectroscopy & Reaction Dynamics

Our research bridges experimental chemical physics—supersonic molecular beams, RF ion guides, and coincidence Velocity Map Imaging—with rigorous quantum-mechanical modeling of polyatomic ions.

Theme 01

BBR-Assisted Laser Cooling of Polyatomic Molecular Ions

Unlike atomic ions, polyatomic molecular ions possess dense rotational and vibrational manifolds that complicate closed optical cycling. We investigate laser-induced, blackbody-radiation (BBR)-assisted rovibrational cooling in symmetric-top cations (NH₃⁺ and ND₃⁺) as well as linear triatomic cations (CO₂⁺ and OCS⁺) exhibiting Renner-Teller vibronic coupling. By driving selected ro-vibrational transitions and leveraging ambient BBR redistribution, internal populations are funneled into the lowest rotational states.

Symmetric-Top Ions (NH₃⁺ / ND₃⁺) Linear Triatomics (CO₂⁺ / OCS⁺) Rate-Equation Modeling
Theme 02

Dual-Sided Coincidence Velocity Map Imaging (VMI)

We are developing a dual-sided Velocity Map Imaging spectrometer capable of detecting positive ions and electrons/negative ions in coincidence. Equipped with precision electrostatic immersion lenses, dual synchronized gigabit CMOS cameras (Basler ace), and real-time sub-pixel centroiding software (VMI Studio Pro), the apparatus maps 3D velocity spheres of photofragments and collision products with high kinetic energy resolution.

Dual-Sided VMI Positive/Negative Coincidence Sub-Pixel Centroiding
Theme 03

Non-Linear Resonances in Quadrupole Mass Filters (QMF)

Mechanical imperfections or intentional electrode asymmetries in Quadrupole Mass Filters introduce higher-order multipole fields (hexapole, octupole, dodecapole) on top of the ideal quadrupolar potential. Using custom SIMION 8.1/2020 user programs and Python multipole expansions, we map Mathieu stability islands, secular frequency shifts, and non-linear resonance lines in single-rod and dual-rod asymmetric QMFs.

Mathieu Stability Diagrams Multipole Expansions Asymmetric QMF
Theme 04

Supersonic Metastable Beams & Cold Collision Dynamics

Crossed-molecular-beam and guided-ion-beam experiments probe low-temperature Penning ionization and chemi-ionization processes involving metastable helium (He*) and argon (Ar). We design high-voltage RF drivers for Dielectric Barrier Discharge (DBD) plasma sources mounted directly on Even-Lavie pulsed valves to produce intense, cold supersonic packets of metastable atoms and molecular ions.

Even-Lavie Pulsed Valve DBD Plasma Source Penning Ionization

Methodology Deep Dive

Theoretical & Experimental Principles

By coupling Einstein A and B coefficients for spontaneous emission, ambient blackbody radiation (BBR) absorption/stimulated emission, and laser-driven vibrational pumping into a master rate-equation solver, we simulate time-dependent population dynamics across hundreds of rovibrational levels. In symmetric-top ions (NH₃⁺, ND₃⁺), symmetry constraints preserve nuclear-spin modifications while cooling rotational states; in linear triatomic cations (CO₂⁺, OCS⁺), Renner-Teller vibronic coupling and spin-orbit splitting govern transition dipole moments and cooling efficiency.

When one or two rods of a four-rod Quadrupole Mass Filter are radially or angularly displaced, higher-order spatial harmonics (A₃ hexapole, A₄ octupole, etc.) couple the transverse x and y motions. Whenever the secular oscillation frequencies satisfy the resonance condition nₓβₓ + nᵧβᵧ = 2k, ions gain transverse kinetic energy and are ejected, creating well-defined resonance dips inside the first and second Mathieu stability regions.

Each ion or electron strike on the Chevron MCP stack produces a localized light burst on the phosphor screen imaged at 10–60 Hz by synchronized Basler gigabit Ethernet cameras. Real-time connected-component thresholding and intensity-weighted center-of-mass (COM) calculation achieve sub-pixel spatial resolution, dramatically sharpening the reconstructed 3D velocity distribution after inverse Abel transformation.

Principal Investigator

Dr. Nabanita Deb

Faculty, School of Chemical Sciences · Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science (IACS), Kolkata

Dr. Nabanita Deb leads the Cold Ion & Molecular Dynamics Group at IACS Kolkata. Prior to joining the faculty at IACS, she conducted pioneering research on laser-cooled Coulomb crystals, sympathetic cooling of molecular ions, and cold ion–molecule reaction dynamics at leading international institutions including the University of Oxford and the Weizmann Institute of Science.

At IACS, her research group focuses on low-temperature collision dynamics, dual-sided Velocity Map Imaging (VMI) coincidence spectrometry, Quadrupole Mass Filter ion optics, and blackbody-radiation-assisted laser cooling of polyatomic molecular ions.
