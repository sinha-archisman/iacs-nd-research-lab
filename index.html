Core Capabilities

Precision Atomic & Molecular Physics

By combining radiofrequency ion trapping, Doppler laser cooling, and 3D momentum imaging, we isolate cold molecular species in well-defined quantum states to probe fundamental spectroscopy and astrochemically relevant reactions.

Cold Molecular Ion Spectroscopy

Co-trapping target molecular ions alongside laser-cooled atomic ions (such as Ca⁺) transfers kinetic energy via long-range Coulomb interactions, sympathetically cooling molecules down to millikelvin temperatures for Doppler-free vibrational and electronic action spectroscopy.

Sympathetic Cooling Action Spectroscopy Astrochemistry

Laser Cooling & Coulomb Crystals

Using narrow-linewidth CW diode lasers at 397 nm and 866 nm stabilized to a high-precision optical wavemeter, we cool trapped atomic ions until their thermal energy falls well below their mutual Coulomb repulsion—forming spatially ordered 3D Wigner crystals.

Ca⁺ Wigner Crystals 397 nm / 866 nm Lasers Single-Ion Imaging

Velocity Map Imaging (VMI) & TOF-MS

Our custom electrostatic lens stack projects charged photofragments and reaction products onto a position-sensitive MCP/phosphor screen detector, reconstructing full 3D velocity distributions and branching ratios with single-Dalton mass resolution.

3D Momentum Reconstruction Time-of-Flight MS Photodissociation

Cold Ion–Molecule Reaction Dynamics

Investigating barrierless ion–molecule collisions at temperatures between 10 mK and 10 K where quantum tunneling, long-range Stark capture, and rotational state-selectivity dominate—mimicking conditions in interstellar clouds and planetary atmospheres.

Our Mission at IACS

Bridging Atomic Physics & Quantum Chemistry in India

Situated at the historic Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science (IACS) in Jadavpur, Kolkata—Asia's oldest research institute where Sir C. V. Raman discovered the Raman Effect—our laboratory develops state-of-the-art hybrid ion-trapping and imaging instrumentation from the ground up.

We train PhD scholars, postdoctoral fellows, and integrated MSc students in ultra-high vacuum engineering, narrow-linewidth laser optics, RF electronics, and computational molecular dynamics simulation.
