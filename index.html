Explore Our Research

About Our Laboratory

Our research group focuses on precision spectroscopy, molecular dynamics, and advanced instrumentation design. Utilizing state-of-the-art ultra-high-vacuum systems, velocity map imaging (VMI) spectrometers, and custom electronics, we investigate the fundamental behavior of polyatomic ions and their interactions under controlled environments.

Research Thrust Areas

Cold Molecular Ions

Investigating blackbody-radiation-assisted laser cooling schemes, rovibrational energy state distributions, and optical pumping techniques for polyatomic cations.

Velocity Map Imaging

Developing dual-sided VMI setups and high-speed CMOS camera synchronizations for coincidence detection and high-resolution ion dynamics visualization.

Ion Optics & Instrumentation

Simulating and optimizing asymmetric quadrupole mass filters, high-voltage pulsed valves, and custom RF driver electronics using SIMION and KiCad.
