Milestones & Updates

Lab News, Grants & Scientific Events

Follow our chronological timeline of experimental milestones, student achievements, research grants, and international conferences.

October 2026 Recruitment

PhD & Postdoctoral Positions Open for 2026–2027

We are inviting applications from highly motivated candidates with backgrounds in Physics or Physical Chemistry (CSIR-NET / GATE / JEST / INSPIRE) to join our experimental cold molecular ion spectroscopy program at IACS Kolkata.

August 2026 Instrumentation Milestone

Commissioning of the VMI Electrostatic Lens Stack & MCP Detector

Our custom Velocity Map Imaging (VMI) detector assembly and fast HV push-pull extraction switches achieved sub-Dalton mass resolution in Time-of-Flight operation under Ultra-High Vacuum (< 10⁻¹⁰ mbar).

May 2026 International Collaboration

Joint Research Exchange with University of Liverpool

Collaborative benchmarking of trajectory simulations and RF trap extraction timing protocols conducted with our academic partners at the Department of Physics, University of Liverpool.

February 2026 Laser Cooling

Automated Multi-Channel Wavemeter PID Lock for 397 nm & 866 nm ECDLs

Long-term frequency stability (< 5 MHz drift over 8 hours) demonstrated on our calcium Doppler cooling and repump diode laser system, enabling continuous multi-hour Coulomb crystal trapping.

2024–2025 Research Grant

Extramural & Institutional Start-Up Grants Awarded

Major equipment funding secured through IACS Kolkata and national funding agencies (ANRF / DST-SERB) to establish the Cold Ion & Molecular Spectroscopy facility.
