Experimental Infrastructure

Custom Instrumentation for Quantum-State Control

Our laboratory at IACS Kolkata houses custom-designed Ultra-High Vacuum (UHV) chambers, precision-machined radiofrequency ion traps, frequency-stabilized CW and pulsed laser systems, and 3D particle imaging detectors.

assets/images/uhv-chamber.jpg

UHV Linear Paul Trap Chamber

Multi-stage differentially pumped 316LN stainless steel vacuum system equipped with turbomolecular, ion getter, and non-evaporable getter (NEG) pumps to prevent background gas collisions with trapped Coulomb crystals over hours of trapping time.

  • Base Pressure< 2 × 10⁻¹⁰ mbar
  • Trap GeometrySegmented 4-Rod Linear Paul Trap
  • Optical AccessHigh-NA Re-entrant UV Fused Silica Viewports
  • Neutral Gas IntroductionPiezo / Pulsed Valve Skimmed Molecular Beam
assets/images/vmi-detector.jpg

Velocity Map Imaging (VMI) & TOF Spectrometer

Custom electrostatic immersion lens assembly integrated directly with the ion trap to project ions along a mu-metal shielded Time-of-Flight drift tube onto a dual Microchannel Plate (MCP) and fast P47 phosphor screen.

  • Detector Stack40 mm Chevron Dual MCP + P47 Phosphor
  • Optical ReadoutHigh-Speed CMOS Camera + Fast PMT
  • Mass GatingNanosecond HV Push-Pull Pulser
  • Fluorescence ImagingHigh-Sensitivity EMCCD Camera + 10× Objective
assets/images/laser-table.jpg

Narrow-Linewidth CW & Pulsed Laser Suite

Vibration-isolated optical tables housing external-cavity diode lasers (ECDLs) for Ca+ Doppler cooling, repumping, and photoionization, alongside tunable UV/visible/IR sources for molecular spectroscopy.

  • Ca⁺ Cooling & Repump397 nm, 866 nm & 854 nm Grating-Stabilized ECDLs
  • Neutral Ca Photoionization423 nm + 375 nm Two-Photon Ionization
  • Frequency StabilizationHigh-Finesse Wavelength Meter (±10 MHz PID Lock)
  • Beam ConditioningAOM/EOM Switching & Single-Mode PM Fiber Delivery
assets/images/rf-electronics.jpg

Phase-Locked RF & FPGA Timing Electronics

Low-noise radiofrequency trap drives with active amplitude stabilization and fast HV switching circuitry enabling rapid transition from RF trapping mode to electrostatic VMI extraction mode within a fraction of an RF cycle.

  • Trap RF Drive1–4 MHz Phase-Locked Sinusoidal Drive (up to 600 Vpp)
  • HV Extraction Switches< 25 ns Rise-Time Behlke MOSFET Switches
  • Experimental TimingFPGA Pulse Generator (1 ns Resolution)
  • Data AcquisitionCustom Python / LabVIEW Control & MD Simulation Cluster
